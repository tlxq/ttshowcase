# Brief

## Who
Tom Larsson, fullstack engineer: TypeScript, React and C#/.NET. I build and run things myself, from data model to server.

## What
My portfolio, at ttdevs.com. One page. The word `tlxq` is the whole site: t Tom, l Live, x eXperiments, q Questions. English only.

The handwriting is mine. I write the words on the phone with the S Pen, and they become tags: tlxq, hi I'm Tom, try it, still running, my own server, my daily tools, work in progress, ask me, the 404 and the tab icon.

## Who it's for
Someone hiring, who has one minute and many other tabs open. They should remember this site, and they should find the facts fast.

## The feeling
Handcrafted and mind-blowing. Four big moments, everything else calm:
1. The first screen. My tlxq tag writes itself in spray paint. Its last stroke is the line, and it carries on down the page.
2. The four letters of `tlxq` fly out, lock together into one word and tip away.
3. Each live project is a case study. Its name rises, a laptop opens, a phone slides out from behind it.
4. The end. The questions slide in, and the line turns into my signature.

Small things join in: a marker for a cursor, paint under the words you select, and a tab icon that drips while you are on another tab.

## Tone
Plain, first person, specific. Real projects and provable facts. No slogans, no filler.

## Never
- Text or components that look generated or come from a template.
- Stock photos or made-up data on the real site. Demo screens use demo data only.
- Anything on the private never-public list, which stays outside this repo.

## Done means
- Live over HTTPS, with the link in my CV.
- Every public repo has passed the public check.
- Works on phone and desktop, proven on the real site.
- Reduced motion still gives a complete page with all the text.
- Lighthouse mobile: performance at least 85, accessibility at least 95.
