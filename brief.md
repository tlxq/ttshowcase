# Brief

## Who
Tom Larsson, product engineer: TypeScript, React and C#/.NET. I build and run things myself, from data model to server.

## What
My portfolio, at ttdevs.com. English only. One page, scrolled from top to bottom: first who I am and what I built, then the graffiti walk, where a visitor can leave a tag.

## Who it's for
Someone hiring, who has one minute and many other tabs open. They should remember this site, and they should find the facts fast.

## The front
1. My name, my role and my photo. A blue ring circles my head, and the photo turns a little with the mouse.
2. The photo shrinks and turns black and white, my builds' names run behind it, and the ring unwinds into my signature.
3. One line about how I work: I build and run things myself, from data model to server.
4. My work: epaRadar, raddavarfruberga and EDGE, as real screens on a track that moves sideways, each with its line, its stack and its links.
5. The end: write to me, GitHub and LinkedIn.

The blue changes form at every moment: ring, signature, paint, ink behind each project, and a dot at the end. The Direction page says how; it is private, and its link is in my plan.

## The walk
The walk comes after the front. How the front leads into it is picked when the real site is built.

A bucket of paint waits on a dark wall at night. The visitor tips it, the paint pours down the wall and becomes the line, and the line leads past my builds.
1. The opening wall at night. The visitor tips the bucket, the paint pours, and the street light comes on.
2. epaRadar and raddavarfruberga: the facts and links sprayed through stencils onto their walls.
3. The workshop: my own tools in a row: ttoolbox, dot and tthermal, painted as one long piece on the workshop wall.
4. The building site: client work for EDGE Associates, painted on a site container: edgeflow, with its links.
5. Dawn grows to white, and out of the white you are back at the opening wall, in your own light (day or night by your clock): the mural and the visitors' tags round it. The can at the mural's foot lets a visitor paint their tag on the wall. Scrolling on, the walk goes round again.
6. Write to me is a sticker on the street light's pole, among stickers about me. Tap it and it unfolds into a flyer: a form to write to me, and my email, GitHub and LinkedIn. The same sticker sits in the screen's corner the whole way. Scrolling up from the start walks round the pole, close enough to read the stickers, on to the visitors' tags.

Every screen has something moving and some depth. The scroll never waits for an animation.

## Tags
No tags on the walls yet. Visitors leave one tag each, and it shows once I approve it. I add my own tags later, as admin.

## Tone
Plain, first person, specific. Real projects and provable facts. No slogans, no filler.

## Never
- Text or components that look generated or come from a template.
- Stock photos or made-up data on the real site. Demo screens use demo data only.
- Anything on the private never-public list, which stays outside this repo.

## Done means
- Live over HTTPS, with the link in my CV.
- Every public repo has passed the public check.
- Works on phone and desktop, proven on the real site.
- Reduced motion still gives a complete page with all the text.
- Lighthouse mobile: performance at least 85, accessibility at least 95.
